## What Karsa Can Build

- Ongoing bug fixes and technical maintenance
- Dependency, security, and platform updates
- Performance monitoring and optimization
- Incremental feature development after launch

## Process

Maintenance sits in the Grow stage of the Karsa Method — the product has already shipped, so this stage focuses on keeping it reliable and steadily improving it based on real usage.
