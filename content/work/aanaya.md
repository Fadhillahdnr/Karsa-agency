## Overview

Aanaya is an independent e-commerce concept built end-to-end — UI/UX design through to a working web implementation — to explore what a genuinely focused shopping experience looks like when scope is treated as a design constraint rather than an afterthought.

## Context

Most small commerce builds accumulate complexity: category trees, filter panels, bundled promotions. Aanaya was scoped deliberately to avoid that, testing how much of a commerce flow could be simplified without losing usability.

## Approach

Design and engineering moved together rather than in separate phases. Layout, interaction, and data flow were built as one connected system — product listing, product detail, and checkout share the same visual and technical language throughout.
